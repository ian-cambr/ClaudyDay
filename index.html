Claude Code keeps its stats on the computer you ran it on, inside the hidden .claude folder in your home folder. Drop in stats-cache.json (what /stats reads), or any session logs (.jsonl) from .claude/projects. Several files at once are added together.

How the cups are estimated
  • Energy per 1,000 tokens: 0.96 Wh output, 0.32 Wh input, 0.40 Wh cache write, 0.032 Wh cache read, plus 10% data-centre overhead (per-token method built on Watershed's framework).
  • Water per Wh: 3.6 mL at US averages including power plants, or 1.15 mL for cooling only (Google's measured rate).
  • 1 burger = about 2,400 L (the full supply-chain water footprint of a 150 g beef burger). 1 chocolate bar (100 g, dark chocolate is vegan) = about 1,700 L, mostly the water to grow cocoa. 1 toilet roll = about 6 US gallons (23 L) to manufacture; some estimates go as high as 37 gallons.
  • 1 cup = 236.6 mL (US cup). Anthropic publishes no per-token water figures, so treat this as an order-of-magnitude estimate.
  • Sources: Google, Gemini footprint paper, Watershed AI emissions framework, Mistral lifecycle analysis.
